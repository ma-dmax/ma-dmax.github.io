About me

I am an entry-level software developer based in Sacramento, California. I have completed the degree requirements for an A.S. in Computer Science — requirements completed at Sacramento City College and am continuing coursework toward a Web Developer A.S. — in progress and university transfer.

My strongest current work is in web development: building responsive interfaces, working with browser APIs and asynchronous data, and turning a small idea into a usable project with real controls and states. I am not tied to one language or stack. I am especially interested in web and full-stack work, but I am open to entry-level software and technical roles where I can help the team, learn quickly, and keep building stronger systems.
